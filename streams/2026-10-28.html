SOXL arithmetic clarification

The $8.45 and $35 credits are one put each, confirmed at 17:59–18:57 and 1:04:04–1:04:49. Average put premium is $21.725/share; assignment basis for those 200 shares is $113.275/share. With 200 shares at $90, the combined 400-share cash basis would be $101.6375 before the call credit, or $100.275 if the call expires and both puts assign. Early spoken estimates of $116–117 are superseded by this exact calculation. The 400-share scenario is conditional, not current ownership. The $120 call/$135 put strikes overlap; they do not create a no-assignment zone.

A 20% stock decline requires a 25% rebound to recover the starting price (50% to reach 20% above that original price); the spoken “40%” shorthand is not used as return arithmetic. Covered calls and new puts do not establish a minimum total-position return.
